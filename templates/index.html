About MasterMind AI

MasterMind AI is an explainable credit scoring system built on the Home Credit Default Risk dataset.

The pipeline runs through five modules: data preparation, feature engineering, model training, calibration, and a live Flask API.

Every decision from the API includes a calibrated probability of default, a policy outcome - APPROVE, REVIEW, or DECLINE - and the top five SHAP feature drivers.
